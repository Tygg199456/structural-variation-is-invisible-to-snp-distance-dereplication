## Dual-use research of concern

No new isolates were obtained, no laboratory work was performed, and no clinical
samples were used: every genome analysed here was already publicly deposited before
this study began. *Yersinia pestis* is a Tier 1 Select Agent, and all content of this
paper concerns the identification of interpretation errors in that existing public
data. Every reference to plasmid or *pgm* loss concerns attenuation and the
interpretation of detection assays; nothing here describes or facilitates the
construction of a strain of enhanced virulence or the evasion of a vaccine or
diagnostic.
